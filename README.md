# FIFA World Cup Guesser ⚽🏆

A modern FIFA World Cup prediction and fan engagement platform built for football supporters worldwide.

## Features

### Fan Club System

* Join your favorite national team
* View supporters of the same country
* Team supporter statistics
* Country-based fan communities

### World Cup Predictions

* Predict the tournament champion
* Predict the runner-up
* Predict the final score
* Golden Boot predictions
* Golden Ball predictions
* Most goals scored predictions
* Biggest tournament disappointment predictions

### Community Insights

* View prediction statistics
* Explore fan distribution by country
* Discover the most supported teams

## Technology Stack

### Frontend

* React
* Vite
* Tailwind CSS
* Framer Motion

### Backend

* Node.js
* Express.js

### Database

* MongoDB Atlas

### Hosting

* Vercel
* Render

## Vision

The goal of FIFA World Cup Guesser is to create a simple and engaging platform where football fans can unite behind their favorite national teams and share their predictions before the world's biggest football tournament.
